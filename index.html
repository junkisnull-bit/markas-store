About Markaz

Markaz is your go-to home essentials store based in Islamabad, delivering quality products for your kitchen, crockery, cleaning, décor and daily needs — right to your door across Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves quality home products at affordable prices without the hassle of shopping in traffic. We source carefully, price fairly, and deliver fast.

Why Markaz?

  • Wide range of home essentials in one place
  • Fast delivery across ISB & RWP
  • Cash on delivery — no upfront payment needed
  • 6-day easy return policy
  • Friendly customer support on WhatsApp

Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy, we're here to help.

6-Day Return Window

You have 6 days from the date of delivery to request a return or refund. After 6 days, we unfortunately cannot offer a refund or exchange.

Conditions for Return

  • Item must be unused and in its original condition
  • Item must be in original packaging
  • Proof of purchase (order ID) is required
  • Damaged or defective items are eligible for full refund

How to Request a Return

Contact us on WhatsApp at 0311-8885604 with your order ID and reason for return. We will arrange pickup at no extra cost for defective items.

Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be processed within 3-5 business days via cash or bank transfer.

Shipping Information

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver across Islamabad and Rawalpindi only.

Delivery Options

  • Standard Delivery — 2 to 3 working days
  • Express Delivery — Same day or next day (order before 12pm for same-day). Additional Rs. 100 charge applies.

Delivery Fees

  • Islamabad (F, G, E sectors, DHA, Bahria) — Rs. 150
  • Rawalpindi (Saddar, Westridge, Chaklala, PWD, CBR) — Rs. 200
  • Other areas in ISB/RWP — Rs. 250

Payment

We accept Cash on Delivery only. Pay when you receive your order — no advance payment needed.

Order Processing

Orders placed before 12pm are processed the same day. Orders after 12pm are processed the next business day.
